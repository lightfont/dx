Dx/
Clinical reasoning trainer · NUS Medicine Year 2
Phase 1
Build Your Differential Diagnosis
Read the vignette. Browse the diagnosis library on the left — every diagnosis in your preclinical syllabus. Select your tag (Must Not Miss / Working Diagnosis) then click to add. Think broadly — missing key diagnoses here is the most penalised error.
Phase 2
History → Examination → Investigations (locked order)
Work through stages sequentially. You can skip any stage but it costs marks. Irrelevant options return "No significant findings." After each stage, rank your differential by likelihood. You can also eliminate diagnoses at any point during Phase 2.
Phase 3
Commit & Debrief
Select your final diagnosis and specify the subtype for full marks. The debrief reveals teaching points, must-not-miss rule-outs, and expert commentary on your pathway.
🔴 Hard rule: Advancing without all must-not-miss diagnoses on your list triggers a safety stop and a permanent −30 pt penalty. Forcing through costs −50 pts. Safe clinical reasoning is the primary skill this game trains.
🎯 Approach (30%)
MNM coverage + common DDx breadth + significant negatives
⚙️ Process (40%)
History weighted most, then PE, then investigations
✅ Diagnosis (30%)
Specific subtype > umbrella > wrong diagnosis
