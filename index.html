Clinical reasoning trainer · NUS Medicine Year 2
Phase 1
Build Your Differential
Read the vignette. Browse the diagnosis library on the left — it contains diagnoses from all body systems in your preclinical curriculum. Add everything you'd seriously consider. Tag each as Must Not Miss (dangerous to overlook) or Working Diagnosis (most likely). Think broadly — missing key diagnoses at this stage is the most penalised error in the game.
Phase 2
History → Examination → Investigations (in order)
Work through stages in sequence. You can skip any stage, but that will cost you marks. Within each stage, select what you want to do — irrelevant options return "No significant findings." After completing each stage, you'll rank your differential by likelihood. History is weighted most heavily.
Phase 3
Commit & Debrief
Select your final diagnosis. If your umbrella is correct (e.g. "Myocardial Infarction"), specify the subtype for full marks. The debrief reveals what this case was teaching, which diagnoses were must-not-miss, and what ruled each one in or out.
🔴 Hard rule: If you advance to History without all must-not-miss diagnoses on your list, the case stops and explains why each missed diagnosis matters clinically. Going back adds a −30 pt penalty; forcing through costs more. We train safe doctors first.
🎯 Approach (30%)
Must-not-miss coverage, common DDx breadth, significant negatives
⚙️ Process (40%)
History thoroughness, targeted PE, appropriate investigations
✅ Diagnosis (30%)
Specific subtype > umbrella > wrong diagnosis
