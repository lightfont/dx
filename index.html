Phase 1
Build Your Differential
Read the vignette. Use the VITAMIN CDE scaffold to think systematically. Search for diagnoses and add them — tag each as Must Not Miss or Working Diagnosis. Selecting an umbrella diagnosis (e.g. "Myocardial Infarction") includes all its subtypes. You can drag entries to rank them by likelihood.
Phase 2
Investigate & Rank
Choose what to ask, examine, or test. After each finding is revealed, drag your differential into order — most likely at the top. This is Bayesian reasoning: each new finding updates your probabilities. Eliminate subtypes or whole diagnoses as evidence accumulates. Irrelevant tests cost efficiency points; high-cost tests cost more.
Phase 3
Commit & Debrief
Commit to your single final diagnosis. The debrief shows your badges, score breakdown, expert reasoning at each step, how your ranking evolved vs the expert, and sensitivity/specificity lessons from the case.
🔴 Hard rule: If you proceed to Phase 2 without a must-not-miss diagnosis on your list, the case pauses and explains the clinical consequence. You can go back and add it, or continue — but you forfeit the Safety Net badge and it's recorded in your debrief.
🛡️ Safety Net — all MNMs on differential
⚡ Efficient — minimal irrelevant tests
🎯 Precision — correct final diagnosis
🧠 Reasoner — correct ranking progression
