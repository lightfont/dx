Clinical reasoning trainer · NUS Medicine Year 2
Phase 1
Build Your Differential Diagnosis
Read the vignette. Open the diagnosis library and add conditions using the MNM or WD buttons. Think broadly — missing key diagnoses here is the most penalised error.
Phase 2
History → Examination → Investigations
Work through stages sequentially. Skipping costs marks. After each stage, rank your differential by likelihood — this trains Bayesian reasoning.
Phase 3
Commit & Debrief
Select your final diagnosis and specify the subtype for full marks. The debrief reveals teaching points, key pivots, and expert commentary.
🔴 Hard rule: Advancing without all must-not-miss diagnoses triggers a safety stop and a permanent penalty. Safe clinical reasoning is the primary skill this game trains.

Diagnosis (30%)
